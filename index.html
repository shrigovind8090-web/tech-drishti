About Tech Drishti

Tech Drishti is an independent online platform. It brings live news in many Indian languages, weather for wherever you are, a free AI assistant, market rates and instant knowledge together in one professional, accessible place.

It is fully online — everything is fetched fresh from public sources — and it is free, with nothing to install. You can sign in with Google or an email address to keep your profile with you, or simply continue as a guest. The accessibility mode is a first-class part of the platform, designed so visually impaired users can use every feature comfortably.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

Tech Drishti is committed to protecting your privacy. This page explains, in plain words, exactly what happens to your information.

1. Using the site without an account

You can use Tech Drishti as a guest, without any account at all. In guest mode we receive nothing about you.

2. If you sign in

If you choose to sign in — with Google, or with an email address and password — we receive your name, your email address and, for Google sign-in, your profile photo. Nothing else.

Your account is created and stored by Firebase Authentication, a service of Google. Your password is handled by Firebase: Tech Drishti never sees it and never stores it.

3. What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone. There are no advertisements, no trackers and no third-party analytics on this site.

4. What stays on your own device

Your preferences — theme, language, text size, the city you chose, your AI assistant conversation and your saved notes — are stored only in your own browser. They never leave your device.

5. Live data requests

To show live news, weather, currency and market prices, your browser requests public data directly from third-party services (listed under Data Sources). As with any website you visit, those services may see your IP address. Tech Drishti keeps no logs of its own.

6. Location

Your location is used only when you press a "My location" button, only to fetch your local weather and news, and only inside your browser. You can always type a city name instead, and you can use the whole site without ever sharing your location.

7. Deleting your account

You can sign out at any time from the Profile tab. To have your account and its data deleted completely, write to us at shrigovind8090@gmail.com and we will remove it.

8. Children

Tech Drishti is suitable for all ages and contains no advertising or adult content.

9. Changes

Any future change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
