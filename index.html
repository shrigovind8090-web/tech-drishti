What makes Tech Drishti accessible

Every screen uses proper landmarks, headings and ARIA labels so screen readers announce it correctly. Interactive controls are reachable and operable by keyboard, focus outlines are always visible, and there is a skip-to-content link at the top.

The whole page can be read aloud, headlines and weather included. Text size, contrast, spacing and motion can all be adjusted from this screen — your choices are remembered on your device.

About Tech Drishti

Tech Drishti is the main platform of the Tech Drishti family, built by Shri Govind. It brings live news in many Indian languages, weather for wherever you are, a free AI assistant, market rates and instant knowledge together in one professional, accessible place.

It is fully online — everything is fetched fresh from public, key-free sources — and it needs no installation, no login and no payment. The accessibility mode is a first-class part of the platform, designed so visually impaired users can use every feature comfortably.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

Tech Drishti, developed by Shri Govind, is committed to protecting your privacy.

1. No personal data collection

Tech Drishti does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information. There is no account requirement and no tracking.

2. Local storage only

Your name, preferences and settings are stored locally on your own device. They never leave it.

3. Live data requests

To show live news, weather and market rates, your browser requests public data directly from third-party services (listed below). Those services may see your IP address, as with any website you visit. Tech Drishti itself runs no server and keeps no logs.

4. Location

Location is used only when you press a "My location" button, only to fetch your local weather and news, and only inside your browser. You can always type a city name instead.

5. Safe for all

No ads, no trackers, no third-party cookies. Any future change to this policy will be reflected on this page.
